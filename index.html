Real Tax Knowledge

Tax Tips From Your Financial Partner

The same guidance Raven shares with her community — because informed clients keep more of their money.

Withholding, Explained

Your W-4 tells your employer how much federal tax to withhold. Too little means a surprise bill in April. Too much means a smaller paycheck all year. The goal isn't just a refund — it's getting it right.

Track Every Dollar

Keeping income and expense records year-round catches every deduction, keeps you audit-ready, and separates business from personal. Five minutes a week beats a shoebox in March.

When to Amend a Return

Missed income, the wrong filing status, overlooked deductions or credits, or a corrected W-2/1099 — any of these can mean money left on the table. An amendment can fix it.

Know Your Write-Offs

Self-employed? Home office, mileage, supplies, software, marketing, and insurance may all be deductible. Proper documentation turns everyday spending into tax savings.

Common Questions

Before You Ask

Do I have to live in Atlanta to work with you?

No — NTS serves clients in all 50 states. Most of our work happens virtually: documents upload securely, and we meet by phone or video. If you're in the Atlanta area, in-person appointments are available.

What should I bring to my tax appointment?

Photo ID, last year's return if you have one, all W-2s and 1099s, records of deductible expenses, and Social Security cards for you and any dependents. Self-employed? Bring your income and expense records — we'll help organize them.

I got a letter from the IRS. Can you help?

Yes — don't ignore it and don't panic. We offer IRS transcript reviews, tax problem resolution, and Offer in Compromise education. Bring or upload the notice and we'll walk through your options together.

Do you offer refund advances?

Yes — refund-advance options up to $2,500 may be available when your return is filed, subject to eligibility and bank approval. Ask about current options at your appointment.

I'm self-employed or paid in cash. Can you still file my taxes?

Absolutely — that's one of our specialties. We help stylists, contractors, gig workers, and cash-income earners document income properly so your return is accurate and audit-ready.
